### Welcome to my github!

#### About me:
Currently, I work as a ML Engineer at Annapurna Labs, Amazon's in-house custom silicon lab.

I’m a graduate of MIT’s Master’s of Business Analytics (MBAn) program, with a focus on Machine Learning, AI, and Data Science at MIT’s Operations Research Center. At MIT, I conducted research advised by Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas and worked for Lineage Logistics and Pfizer. Previously, I studied Systems Engineering, Government, and Computer Science at the University of Virginia. 

#### Interests:
AI/ML, optimization, healthcare improvement, logistics optimization, and data-driven policymaking.
